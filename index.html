Ready for service?

You're the food runner. When the bell rings, a tray appears on the expo pass inside the kitchen with a table number. Go in through the IN door to pick it up, then carry it out to that table.

Wrong table? You'll be told whose table it is — no penalty, just keep running. There's no timer and no game over — train as long as you like.

How to play: Move with WASD or the arrow keys (or the pad). You start inside the kitchen at the top, and the tray waits on the pass inside the kitchen — step up to it to pick it up, then walk into the correct table to deliver it. Tables are solid — work the aisles, just like the real floor. The pass doors are one-way: out of the kitchen through the OUT door on the left, back in through the IN door on the right, so every pickup means a trip back into the kitchen. A half wall runs between the 300s and the 400s — there's no cutting between those rows; go around the top or the bottom of it. The 800s are outside on the patio — reach them through the door at the top of the patio wall, above table 412. Turn on Hint if you want the target table to pulse while you learn the floor.
